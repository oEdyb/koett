# Dictionary word lists

`common-words.txt` is derived from SCOWL 2020.12.07 (English and American words,
levels 10 and 20) by Kevin Atkinson and contributors. Its copyright and
permission notice is in `SCOWL-Copyright.txt`.

`common-words-sv.txt` is derived from Språkbanken Text's Flashback corpora
statistics under CC BY 4.0, as described below.

`common-words-sv.txt` lists the 25,000 most frequent lowercase
word forms in the Flashback forum corpora FLASHBACK-MAT and FLASHBACK-LIVSSTIL,
counted with Språkbanken Text's Korp API on 2026-11-02. Forms used more often
capitalized than lowercase (mostly names) were left out.

Source: Språkbanken Text, University of Gothenburg, "Flashback",
https://spraakbanken.gu.se/en/resources/flashback
