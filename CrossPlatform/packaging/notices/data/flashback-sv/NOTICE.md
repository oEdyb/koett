# Swedish common-word list

`src/dictionary/common-words-sv.txt` lists the 25,000 most frequent lowercase
word forms in the Flashback forum corpora FLASHBACK-MAT and FLASHBACK-LIVSSTIL,
counted with Språkbanken Text's Korp API on 2026-11-02. Forms used more often
capitalized than lowercase (mostly names) were left out.

Source: Språkbanken Text, University of Gothenburg, "Flashback",
https://spraakbanken.gu.se/en/resources/flashback
