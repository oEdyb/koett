Koett patches sherpa-onnx-sys 1.13.5 build.rs to require its pinned ASR-only
native source/target receipt and exclude disabled TTS link dependencies.
The original Apache-2.0 license and raw FFI functions are unchanged.
Koett also adds a sha2 build dependency, verifies the shared-context patch/source/
target and native archive fingerprints before exporting compile-time capability
constants from src/lib.rs. A stale capability fails the build instead of selecting
an unsupported native decoder.
