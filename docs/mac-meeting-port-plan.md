# Port Linux meetings to macOS

Research checked 2026-11-27 against `main` at `48f8be3`. Target the M5 MacBook Air with 24 GB on macOS 26.6.2 and keep the package's macOS 15 minimum. The scope is the meeting and pill work in #199–#209, plus the drawing-paste timing change in #198. Use synthetic or consented fixtures in the checks below.

## What the app owns

The Linux tray, pill and CLI send requests to one meeting controller. It owns capture through sealing. A batch job reads the two journals and commits Markdown. History and vault export can each retry from that committed artifact. On Mac, `KoettController` already owns shortcut dispatch and session admission, `LocalControlSocket` is a same-user Unix socket, and `RecordingOverlayController` owns a nonactivating AppKit panel. The mic comes from a selected-device HAL Audio Unit. [Linux state machine](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/mod.rs) · [Mac controller](../Sources/Koett/Koett.swift) · [Mac control socket](../Sources/Koett/LocalControlSocket.swift) · [Microphone input](../Sources/Koett/MicrophoneInput.swift)

Keep one Swift meeting controller inside the signed menu-bar app. The shortcut sends `toggle`, the pill or menu sends `stop(id)` and then a tokened choice, and the CLI sends the same typed commands. A separate capture daemon would need its own permission and signing story, an extra device owner, and state synchronization with the menu. Keep that complexity inside the existing app and isolate only the resource-heavy decoder in an on-demand child.

```text
Shortcut / menu / pill / local CLI → MeetingController.send(Command) → Reply
                                  ← Snapshot (phase, health, elapsed, levels)
                                    Capture → MeetingBundle ← batch ASR
                                               Markdown → History / vault
```

- One transition function owns `idle → starting → recording ↔ confirmStop → saving → processing → idle`. A meeting UUID plus a stop generation identify a confirmation. A job attempt identifies async completions. Stale events have no effects. The app reserves the mic and pill through sealing, then admits normal dictation again while the one meeting job runs. Sparkle waits for capture and durable sealing. An explicit quit during capture uses the existing quit guard. Shutdown that proceeds seals as interrupted when possible.
- Stop leaves both tracks and drawing live. Finish seals and queues ASR. No transcript (CLI `cancel`) seals and retains the audio for explicit recovery. Continue closes the confirmation without stopping capture. The UI arms Finish and No transcript 500 ms after displaying a new token and checks the token and arming time at press start. Continue acts immediately. Repeated Stop preserves the token. Toggle during confirmation is inert. CLI terminal actions also require the current UUID and token. [Linux controller tests](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/machine.rs) · [Linux press policy](../CrossPlatform/src/linux/meeting_confirm.rs)
- Record under `~/Library/Application Support/Koett/Meetings/<uuid>/`. The bundle owns the manifest, journals, marks, cutoff, seal, job error and hash-checked publication receipt. Commit the cutoff before stopping sources, drain the writers and pending marks to that cutoff, then seal. When intent cannot be committed, stay in confirmation if the failure is known to precede commit. Seal as interrupted if commit is uncertain. Recovery reads only complete checksummed records and uses the saved cutoff or the latest valid track extent. Successful primary publication releases audio by default. Held, interrupted, failed and fallback bundles retain audio. History and vault failures replay independently after restart without another decode. [Linux bundle](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/bundle.rs) · [Publication replay](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/bundle/projections.rs)

## System audio

**Use a private, unmuted Core Audio process tap on a private aggregate device for Other.** It captures output with an audio-specific permission and leaves playback audible. Use the existing mic device resolver and HAL input for You. Add `NSAudioCaptureUsageDescription` and extend the mic usage string. The signed app starts capture after an explicit meeting action. The first recording from an aggregate with a tap triggers the system audio permission prompt. Use the separate microphone grant for You and Screen Recording only when a drawing needs an image. Handle denial or zero-data startup with a clear recovery action. Silent PCM can also mean silence, so it cannot establish denial on its own. [Apple tap sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreaudio/capturing-system-audio-with-core-audio-taps) · [Audio usage key](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsaudiocaptureusagedescription) · [Koett permissions](mac-permissions.md)

Start with a global mixdown excluding Koett's process, tied to the selected output route if needed. Observe default-output, device-alive and format changes. Rebuild the affected source, open a new journal epoch, and mark any lost interval. Keep the other track writing. Own every listener, IOProc, aggregate and tap in one capture lifetime and unwind partial setup in reverse order. Use host timestamps and sample counts for one monotonic 16 kHz timeline, continuously account for clock drift and keep PCM conversion and disk I/O off callbacks. Two mono PCM16 tracks cost about 230 MB per hour before journal overhead. A disconnected route gets an explicit reconnecting state. A disk/write failure ends capture as recoverable. [Apple CATapDescription](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreaudio/catapdescription) · [Linux clock](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/capture/clock.rs)

Make app-specific output a release gate. A default-device tap can miss a call routed separately to AirPods. If the global tap misses a required route, add explicit output selection for Other and use the public device-bound tap API. Keep the Mac's default devices intact. Opening an AirPods mic can change Bluetooth profile, quality and sample rate. Test AirPods output with the built-in mic and with the headset mic, then switch outputs mid-call. ScreenCaptureKit is the contingency if taps cannot support the required route on the signed build. It offers independent audio and microphone outputs, but Apple's display-filter sample uses Screen Recording authorization and may require restart after grant. Validate its exact permission and route behavior before considering it. [Screenpipe's reproduced routing failure](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/issues/4638) · [Apple Bluetooth behavior](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102217) · [Apple ScreenCaptureKit sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-in-macos)

## Local Swedish and English

**Start with whisper.cpp v1.9.6 on Metal, using multilingual large-v3-turbo Q5_0 for language ID and English and KBLab's KB-Whisper large Q5_0 for Swedish.** KBLab publishes native GGML weights with documented Swedish gains, so one engine can implement the Linux routing with no conversion step. Use a signed Swift worker target with a thin whisper.cpp C API binding built from the pinned source. Keep model manifests, download hashes and notices next to that boundary. Fetch separately from inference and test the exact signed package fully offline after provisioning. Keep FluidAudio at 0.15.6 and use its Parakeet v3 INT8 path in the worker as the explicit degraded fallback. A successful fallback records its backend and keeps the audio for retry. [whisper.cpp release](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/releases/tag/v1.9.6) · [C API](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/blob/v1.9.6/include/whisper.h) · [KBLab model and GGML files](https://huggingface.co/KBLab/kb-whisper-large/tree/main) · [Pinned FluidAudio long-form guidance](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/blob/4dbf4f9f9a5ff3a53ade848d7ba4e3df13db859b/Documentation/ASR/LongTranscription.md)

- Run VAD on each track in bounded batches, keeping source timestamps. Use turbo's Swedish/English scores on each speech window. When unpadded speech is under 3 s and the two-language normalized confidence is below 0.99, retry ID with up to 10 s of nearby same-track audio in the batch. Decode only the original padded window in the selected language, trim overlap by word ownership and merge turns by time. Record the selected backend and language per turn. Both tracks can overlap. Within-window code-switching remains an accuracy risk. [Linux router](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/asr_helper.py) · [Windows and timestamp ownership](../CrossPlatform/src/meeting/transcript.rs)
- Give the worker background CPU/I/O QoS. Keep one job and one decode at a time, bound queued audio, and load KB weights on first Swedish window. Pass only bounded windows and model handles to the worker. Prefer in-job model reuse while measured memory permits it. Make cancellation and parent exit stop the worker. Yield between windows to foreground dictation if contention shows up. Background QoS lowers CPU and I/O priority. Measure shared GPU contention separately. [Apple QoS](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/dispatch/dispatch_queue_priority_background)
- Core ML in whisper.cpp offloads the encoder and needs matching converted encoder weights. WhisperKit v1.1.1 offers Core ML, language scores and timestamps, with a separate conversion path for KB. MLX audio v0.6.8 offers Metal inference with separate MLX model artifacts. A FluidAudio upgrade to v0.18.4 expands the model choices. Keep those as alternatives if the chosen path misses its measured targets. Parakeet v3 has Swedish and English, but KBLab reports a Swedish advantage for its own model. Apple SpeechTranscriber uses an explicit supported locale, so a separate routing step would still be needed. [whisper.cpp Core ML](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/blob/v1.9.6/README.md#core-ml-support) · [WhisperKit and conversion](https://github.com/argmaxinc/whisperkittools) · [MLX audio release](https://github.com/Neywa-Labs/mlx-audio/releases/tag/v0.6.8) · [FluidAudio release](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/releases/tag/v0.18.4) · [NVIDIA model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3) · [Apple SpeechAnalyzer](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/277/)
- The older M5 Air clip bake-off measured 26.5× real time and 235 MB peak RSS for KB **Base** Q5_0 (9.83% Swedish WER) and 38.1× and 124 MB for Parakeet v3 (15.69%). These are historical single-track clip results. Upstream M5 Max kernel timings also leave the M5 Air two-track speed and memory open. S3 measures that path before choosing a release pin. [Koett bake-off harness](../Benchmarks/Swedish/README.md) · [Upstream bench log](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/blob/master/scripts/bench-all-gg.txt)

### S3 · Non-UI · Decode, publish, and query

**Check.** `swift test --filter 'Meeting(ASR|Publication|History|Worker)Tests'` and `zsh -n install-macos.sh`. Add a `MeetingASRProfileTests` opt-in fixture that compares routed Metal, turbo only and Parakeet v3 on the same bilingual two-track sample. ## Physical Mac gates

- **H1 · Capture and permission.** On an authorized signed test build, approve and deny mic and system-audio access independently. Confirm normal dictation and relaunch behavior. Play known remote speech with output on speakers, AirPods and a wired/USB route. Switch during recording, unplug/reconnect and try the call app's output independently of the default. Check that playback stays audible, both tracks resume or explain gaps, and mic level does not stand in for remote audio. Check a screen-permission denial leaves the meeting usable.
- **H2 · Real call and recovery.** Record a consented Swedish/English call with short replies, overlap and marks. Use the keyboard, menu, three stop choices and Undo. Compare both tracks and note against the call, then open History and the Obsidian note with its images. Force-quit a test capture, relaunch and recover it. Check VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, focus and hover on the real display and a mixed-DPI arrangement. Inspect empty, recording, confirm, Saving, failure and long-clock states. Check dictation response while the worker runs and confirm ordered image/text paste in the normal receiver.

The release risks are silent per-app routing, loss of synchronization after a device/profile change, missed short language switches, and GPU/thermal contention on the fanless Air. The signed hardware probe and the bilingual profile are the evidence gates. Smoke-test signed worker packaging on macOS 15 before shipping. A completed Markdown receipt and independently replayable projections limit data loss if the process exits during export.
